# Case Files Coloring & Activity Book — Marketing Ideas

Status: **IDEA / DEFERRED UNTIL BOOK IS FINISHED**  
Recorded: October 23, 2026

## Hall of Heroes — Beat the AI Challenge

### Core idea
Market the *Case Files Coloring & Activity Book* with a public challenge: **Can a human complete the entire book and beat the AI?**

People who finish **100% of the book, cover to cover**—all coloring pages and all activities—may submit evidence of completion to earn their chosen name on a dedicated **Hall of Heroes** page at TFUNKERA.COM. “Hall of Fame” is an alternate working title; the current favorite is **Hall of Heroes**.

The publisher intends to **complete the book personally and be the first listed finisher**. This is an intention, not a completed or verified entry.

### Proposed buyer journey (not built)
1. Purchase the paperback through Amazon KDP.
2. Complete every coloring and activity page (100%; no partial-completion listing).
3. Follow a printed link or QR code to the challenge page on TFUNKERA.COM.
4. Enter a chosen public display name and upload photographs or other proof of completion.
5. Have evidence reviewed privately before the public entry is approved.
6. On approval, publish the display name and optional completion date / sequential finisher number to the Hall of Heroes.

**Do not auto-publish unverified claims.** A submission form can automate intake and publishing *after approval*; evidence should not be publicly exposed by default. Consent, privacy, moderation, spam prevention, storage, and clear eligibility rules will require decisions before launch. Do not require participants to publicly disclose legal names.

### Possible campaign copy (draft)
**THINK YOU CAN BEAT THE AI?**  
The Case Files are in your hands. Color every scene, solve every puzzle, crack every code, and finish every page. Prove you completed 100% and earn your place in the **Hall of Heroes** at TFUNKERA.COM.

Note: This is a playful challenge about human creativity and persistence, **not** a claim that AI is unable to solve the activities.

### Why test it
- Differentiate the book with a fun, sharable completion challenge.
- Motivate buyers to finish and post about their progress.
- Connect paperback readers back to the Case Files website.
- Build a recognizable community feature for potential future books.

### Risks / unknowns
- Completion of an approximately 100-page book is a high bar; participation and sales lift are unproven.
- Empty or low-participation Hall of Heroes at first.
- False submissions, labor to review evidence, and the need to protect user-uploaded photos/personal information.
- Do not delay KDP publication or spend extensively on a site feature before validating interest.

### Decision and timing
**Park this concept for now.** No website page, submission button, or automation is approved for implementation yet.

**Revisit only after the book is complete** (and coordinate challenge copy/QR code with final book layout if still feasible before KDP upload). First priority: finish/QA the interior and activities, prepare the paperback and cover, and proceed toward publishing.

When resumed, decide: evidence standard, moderator workflow, consent and display-name rules, page design, book QR/link, and launch messaging. Keep first implementation small and inexpensive.
