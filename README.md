# T Funk Era Case Files Coloring Book

## Project Goal
Create a **30-page black-line coloring book** based on creepy and mysterious case files as a new **income stream** under the T Funk Era brand.

The book should feel like a mysterious archive of unexplained cases rather than a generic coloring book. It should combine eerie subject matter, short case summaries, and clean printable line art suitable for Amazon KDP.

## Core Creative Decisions
- **30 case pages**
- **Black line art only**
- Creepy / eerie cases are encouraged
- Intended as a **sellable KDP product and additional income stream**
- Each case should include:
  - **Case File Number**
  - **Case Title**
  - **Short description: 2–3 sentences**
  - One black-and-white coloring illustration

## Tone
- Creepy
- Mysterious
- Atmospheric
- Intriguing
- Not excessively graphic or grotesque

## Art Direction
- Black lines only
- Clean coloring-book style
- Strong outlines
- Printable and easy to color
- Enough detail to feel premium without becoming visually cluttered
- Illustrations should look like a scene or reconstruction from the case
- Maintain a consistent visual style throughout all 30 pages

## Page Structure
Each case page should contain:

**Case File ###**  
**Case Title**  
**Short Description:** 2–3 sentences

The illustration should be the dominant visual element of the page.

## Product Format
Working format:
- Amazon KDP paperback
- Recommended trim size: **8.5 x 11 in**
- Black-and-white interior
- Art designed with print margins in mind
- Single-sided coloring artwork preferred where practical

## Audience
Primary audience:
- Adults and teens interested in UFOs, cryptids, creepy history, paranormal stories, and unexplained mysteries

Secondary audience:
- General coloring-book buyers who enjoy eerie or unusual subject matter

## Business Purpose
This project is intended to become:
1. A new Amazon KDP product
2. An additional income stream
3. A T Funk Era brand extension
4. A foundation for later themed volumes

Possible later volumes:
- Cryptids
- UFOs
- Strange History
- Haunted / Paranormal Cases

## Production Plan
1. Finalize the **30-case lineup**
2. Lock the standard **page template**
3. Produce **3 sample pages**
4. Review and approve the art style
5. Build the remaining pages
6. Design the front/back cover
7. Prepare the KDP-ready interior and cover files
8. Create the Amazon listing copy, keywords, and launch checklist

## Quality Standard
The finished book should feel cohesive and premium rather than generic. Case descriptions should stay concise and readable, while the art carries most of the personality and atmosphere.
