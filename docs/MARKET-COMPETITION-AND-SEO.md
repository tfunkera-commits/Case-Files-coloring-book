# Market Competition & SEO Positioning

_Last updated: October 7, 2026_

## Purpose

This document records the competitive position of the **Case Files Coloring & Activity Book** and the SEO/metadata strategy to use when preparing the Amazon KDP listing.

The goal is to avoid positioning the book as just another generic "cryptid coloring book." The stronger niche is a hybrid of:

- Cryptids
- UFO / UAP cases
- Unexplained mysteries
- Strange history / real-world case files
- Coloring pages
- Puzzles and activities
- Short factual case summaries
- A connected website archive at TFUNKERA.COM

The book currently has **30 completed Case File coloring pages** and is planned to expand toward roughly **100 total pages** with activities while keeping the coloring pages single-sided.

---

## Competitive Landscape

Research completed October 7, 2026 suggests the following market pattern:

| Segment | Competition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cryptid coloring books | High | Many Bigfoot, Mothman, Loch Ness, and general cryptid coloring books |
| Cryptid mixed activity books | Low to moderate | A smaller number combine coloring with puzzles or trivia |
| Alien / space activity books | Moderate to high | Many are aimed at children and use generic cartoon aliens / space themes |
| Serious UFO / UAP case activity books | Very low | Few books appear centered on recognizable real-world cases |
| Cryptids + UFOs + unexplained mysteries | Very low | Very little direct competition found |
| Case-file style coloring + activities + factual mysteries | Extremely low | No strong one-for-one equivalent was identified in the research |

### Representative competitors / adjacent books

Examples found during research include:

- **Cryptid Activity Book: For Adults** — roughly 50 pages with coloring, crosswords, word searches, and other puzzles centered mainly on cryptids.
- **Legendary Monsters: Cryptids Coloring + Activity Book** — roughly 80 pages, combining cryptids, facts, jokes, coloring, and activities; more kid-oriented.
- **BigFoot Activity Book** — roughly 136 pages of mazes, riddles, word games, coloring, crosswords, and trivia, but focused only on Bigfoot.
- Multiple generic **alien / space activity books** featuring mazes, tracing, dot-to-dot, word searches, Sudoku, and cartoon UFO themes.

### Important conclusion

The crowded market is **generic cryptid coloring**.

The less crowded opportunity is:

> **A case-file themed coloring and activity book covering cryptids, UFO/UAP cases, unexplained mysteries, and strange historical cases, with factual summaries and links back to a larger evidence/source archive.**

That is the positioning we should protect.

---

## Core Differentiator

The book should not be sold merely as:

> "A cryptid coloring book."

The stronger positioning is closer to:

> **An interactive unexplained-mysteries case file collection featuring real-world cryptid reports, UFO/UAP cases, strange history, coloring pages, puzzles, short case summaries, and links to the full Case Files archive.**

Each case can reinforce this identity through:

- Case File number
- Subject title
- Artist's depiction / reconstruction
- 2–3 sentence factual introduction
- Related puzzle or activity where appropriate
- TFUNKERA.COM / QR connection for deeper research

This creates a bridge between an activity book and a mystery / paranormal reference experience.

## Working Title Direction

A strong working direction is:

**Case Files: Cryptids, UFOs & Unexplained Mysteries**

Possible subtitle direction:

**A Coloring & Activity Book of Strange Creatures, UFO/UAP Encounters, Unsolved Cases, Puzzles and Mysterious History**

This is not final copy. Before publishing, we should test wording for:

1. Readability on the cover
2. Amazon search relevance
3. Competitor title patterns
4. KDP metadata rules
5. Avoiding unnecessary keyword repetition

---

## Cover SEO / Shopper Communication

The cover needs to explain the niche within seconds.

The front cover should visibly communicate at least three ideas:

1. **CASE FILES**
2. **CRYPTIDS + UFOs + UNEXPLAINED MYSTERIES**
3. **COLORING & ACTIVITY BOOK**

This is more important than trying to place every possible search phrase on the cover.

Potential visual subjects should include at least one cryptid and one UFO/UAP element so a shopper does not assume the book covers only monsters or only aliens.

---

## Amazon Product Description Direction

The description should emphasize:

- 30 Case File coloring illustrations
- Single-sided coloring-page design
- Cryptids, UFO/UAP encounters, unexplained mysteries, and strange history
- Short factual case introductions
- Mazes, word searches, codebreakers, and other case-themed activities
- Recognizable cases rather than generic fantasy creatures
- Connection to the full online Case Files archive at TFUNKERA.COM
- Suitable for older kids, teens, adults, mystery fans, paranormal fans, UFO enthusiasts, and cryptid fans depending on final content / age positioning

### First-line concept

A strong opening idea:

> **Open the file. Examine the mystery. Color the evidence. Solve the case.**

This can be refined later for the final Amazon listing.

---

## Backend Keyword Strategy

When the KDP listing is created, use the available backend keyword fields for **phrases**, not repeated single words.

Priority should be given to phrases that describe the book accurately while expanding beyond the exact title.

Possible keyword-field concepts:

1. `cryptid paranormal activity book`
2. `UFO UAP unexplained mysteries`
3. `Bigfoot Mothman alien coloring`
4. `mystery case files puzzles`
5. `strange history unsolved cases`
6. `cryptids UFOs coloring activities`
7. `paranormal gifts mystery fans`

**Important:** These are working candidates, not final KDP fields. Amazon's current keyword and metadata rules should be rechecked immediately before publication.

---

## Category Strategy

Because this is a hybrid book, category selection should try to place it where it can rank against the most relevant competition rather than simply choosing the broadest coloring category.

Potential category themes to investigate at upload time:

- Games & Activities / Coloring Books
- Games & Activities / Puzzles
- Juvenile or general-interest paranormal / supernatural categories depending on final age positioning
- UFOs / unexplained phenomena
- Cryptozoology / mysterious creatures where available

Amazon category options can change. Final categories should be checked against the live KDP category picker before publication.

---

## SEO Principle: Do Not Compete Only on "Cryptid Coloring Book"

The most important strategic takeaway from the market research:

**"Cryptid coloring book" is useful as a keyword, but it should not be the entire identity of the product.**

Our stronger combination is:

> **Cryptids + UFO/UAPs + unexplained mysteries + real case-file presentation + coloring + activities + factual summaries.**

That combination is considerably less crowded and gives the project its strongest selling proposition.
