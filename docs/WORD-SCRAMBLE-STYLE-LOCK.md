## Fixed layout — keep identical on WS-001 through WS-005
- Portrait page, white paper, black ink only; ink-efficient, print-friendly.
- Light dossier/file-folder outer border with modest tab at upper left reading **CASE FILES ACTIVITY DIVISION**.
- Dominant title **UNSCRAMBLE THE EVIDENCE** and secondary line **INVESTIGATOR'S WORKSHEET 00X**.
- Small rectangular theme label at top left; small **TOP SECRET** rubber stamp near upper right.
- Brief investigator-themed instructions in a typewriter-style font.
- **12 numbered scrambled terms**, divided into **two columns of six**, separated by a thin vertical rule; each term has a generously spaced answer line.
- Bottom simple bordered **BONUS MYSTERY WORD** panel with clue, scrambled term, and answer line. Optional **one small unobtrusive UFO/subject icon**; no scenic illustrations, forests, beams, oversized magnifying glasses, extra stars, or cartoon decorations.
- Footer **T FUNK ERA • CASE FILES • TFUNKERA.COM**.
- Preserve the same margins, typography hierarchy, widths, spacing, border weight, two-column organization, bonus-box size and footer across all five; variable content must fit rather than force a new design.
- No excessive distressed textures, large black silhouette art, full-page graphics, or comic-book styling.

## Difficulty and puzzles
Mixed/medium difficulty: approximately 12 terms plus one bonus clue per page, combining accessible and challenging vocabulary. Every puzzle must use a legitimate answer and an **exact letter-for-letter anagram**; no added/missing letters, misspellings, or ambiguous unintended solutions. Ensure sufficient answer-line width for long words. Maintain separate answer keys; do not put answers on puzzle pages.

**Important QA:** The AI-rendered WS-001 approved reference includes letter errors in some scrambled terms. It is approved as a **visual layout only**, NOT as proofed print-ready puzzle content. Rebuild final WS-001 with mechanically validated anagrams before publication. Do not silently replicate wrong letters.
